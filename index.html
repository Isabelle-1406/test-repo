Teach the app your signs

Choose a letter, make the sign, then press Record. Take about 50 samples per letter, moving your hand a little between them. Do this once; samples stay in this browser.

Save, load or reset samples

SPACE and DEL are optional gestures you can teach to add a space or delete a letter while signing. J and Z involve movement; teach the pose where the sign ends or starts.
