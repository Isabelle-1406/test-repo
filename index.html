Text

Teach a letter

Pick a letter and press Record. After the 2-second countdown, hold the sign for 2 seconds, shifting your hand slightly. Record each letter 2 to 3 times, ideally with different people, using the hand you will sign with.

Strictness:

Lower means stricter. If a letter is never accepted, raise it. If it types the wrong letter, lower it.

Sentence

Teach a gesture

Press Record, wait for the 2-second countdown, then sign for 1.5 seconds. Record each word about 10 times, ideally with different people.

Strictness:

Lower means stricter. If it never fires, raise it. If it fires on the wrong word, lower it.
